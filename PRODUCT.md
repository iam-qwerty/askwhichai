# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People with a concrete task in mind ("I need to transcribe interviews", "I want to generate product photos") who don't know AskWhichAI tool fits. They arrive with intent, type what they want to do, scan results, and leave for the tool's site. Sessions are short and goal-driven. A secondary admin user curates the tool catalog.

## Product Purpose

AskWhichAI is a searchable directory of AI tools with semantic (meaning-based) search. Success = a visitor describes their task in plain language and lands on the right tool within one search and one or two card scans. The product's value is the quality of matching and the scannability of results, not visual spectacle.

## Brand Personality

Clean, trustworthy, efficient. A quiet, credible utility — like a well-made reference tool. Restrained color, strong hierarchy, honest labels. The interface should disappear into the task of choosing a tool.

## Anti-references

- Gradient-soaked AI-tool aggregator sites with neon glows, glassmorphism cards, and hype copy.
- SaaS landing-page clichés: hero metrics, eyebrow kickers on every section, identical icon-card grids.
- Anything that makes the directory feel like an ad farm rather than a curated reference.

## Design Principles

1. **Results are the interface** — the search bar and the tool grid carry the page; everything else stays quiet.
2. **Scannability over decoration** — name, price, and one-line description must be readable at a glance; motion and texture never compete with content.
3. **Earned familiarity** — standard affordances (cards, badges, buttons) done precisely; no invented controls.
4. **Honest states** — loading, empty, and error states tell the user what's happening and what to do next.
5. **Consistent vocabulary** — the same pricing colors, button shapes, and terms mean the same thing on every screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, visible focus indicators, 44px touch targets, full keyboard operability, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected. Pricing tiers must not rely on color alone (text label always present).
